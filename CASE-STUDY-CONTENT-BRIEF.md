# OpenRangeDevs case study content brief

Use this structure for every case study. Short, specific answers are better than polished marketing copy. Include real numbers whenever the client permits them.

## 1. Project overview

This is the top of the page. It should tell the reader what changed and why the work mattered.

- **Project label:** 2–5 words. Example: “Platform continuity.”
- **Main headline:** 6–12 words. State the business result or important change.
- **Brief:** 45–70 words in one paragraph. Explain what the client needed, what OpenRangeDevs handled, and why it mattered.
- **Client name**
- **Industry**
- **Engagement period:** month/year to month/year, or “Ongoing.”
- **OpenRangeDevs role:** 3–5 short service labels.
- **Hero image:** one wide image. Use a product screen, workflow, dashboard, or project-specific graphic. Recommended size: at least 2000 × 1200 px.

## 2. The situation

Explain the business before the work began.

- **Section headline:** 8–14 words.
- **Paragraph 1:** 70–100 words. Describe how the business or team worked before.
- **Paragraph 2:** 70–100 words. Explain the cost of leaving the problem unresolved: lost time, operating cost, delivery risk, missed revenue, or limited capacity.
- **Supporting image:** one image showing the old workflow, existing product, system diagram, or real working environment. Recommended size: at least 1600 × 1000 px.

## 3. The evidence

Show what made this problem worth solving.

- Provide **3–5 facts** from discovery.
- Each fact should be one sentence, no more than 25 words.
- Useful evidence includes repeated manual work, subscription cost, delayed delivery, handoffs, client complaints, conversion loss, or maintenance risk.
- Include numbers where possible.
- **Optional visual:** a simple before-state diagram, spreadsheet extract, workflow map, or annotated screenshot.

## 4. How we worked

Describe the decisions, not a generic design or development process.

Provide three steps:

1. **Step title:** 3–7 words.
2. **Step explanation:** 35–55 words.

Each step should explain:

- What decision had to be made.
- Who OpenRangeDevs worked with.
- What changed because of that decision.

Add **2–4 working images** across this section. Good options are workshop notes, wireframes, architecture diagrams, early product screens, or release comparisons. Recommended size: at least 1600 px wide.

## 5. The solution in use

Show what was created and how it fits the client’s real operation.

- **Section headline:** 6–12 words.
- **Overview paragraph:** 60–90 words.
- **Key capabilities:** 3–6 items, each 8–18 words.
- Explain who uses it, when they use it, and what it replaces or improves.
- **Images:** 3–6 finished product or system images. Include full screens and close-up details. Recommended size: at least 1800 px wide.

## 6. What changed

State the business outcome.

- **Outcome headline:** 6–12 words.
- **Outcome paragraph:** 60–100 words.
- **Results:** 2–4 measurable outcomes.
- Each result should include a number and a short explanation when available.

Useful measures include:

- Revenue created or protected.
- Subscription or operating costs removed.
- Hours saved per week or month.
- Faster turnaround or release time.
- More clients or jobs handled with the same team.
- Fewer errors, handoffs, or support requests.

If exact numbers are confidential, use an approved range or a specific operational change instead of a vague claim.

## 7. Client perspective

Use one quote that says what it felt like to work with OpenRangeDevs or what changed for the business.

- **Quote:** 35–70 words.
- **Name**
- **Role**
- **Company**
- **Portrait:** optional square image, at least 800 × 800 px.

## 8. Ongoing relationship

Explain what happened after the first release.

- **One paragraph:** 50–80 words.
- Mention maintenance, new releases, support, operational changes, or continued product decisions.
- Be clear about why the ongoing relationship is valuable to the client.
- **Optional image:** a newer release, roadmap view, or current product screen.

## 9. Final content checklist

Before handing the content over, confirm that it includes:

- The client’s real business problem.
- The cost or risk of that problem.
- The decisions OpenRangeDevs helped make.
- The work that was delivered.
- A concrete business result.
- At least 6 strong project images.
- One approved client quote.
- Permission to use the client name, logo, images, quote, and results.
